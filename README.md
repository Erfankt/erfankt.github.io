# Erfan Kefayat — Academic & Technical Portfolio

This repository hosts my public academic and technical portfolio, including
research summaries, methodological projects, and applied work in geospatial
data science and urban systems analysis.

I am a final-year PhD candidate in Public Policy Analysis with a specialization
in spatial–temporal analytics, urban morphology, and AI-driven automation for
public-sector workflows.

## Research Focus
- Urban morphology and sustainability
- Spatial–temporal modeling and clustering
- Street networks and urban systems
- GeoAI and machine learning applications
- Data-driven policy and infrastructure analysis

## Methods & Tools
Python · R · ArcGIS Pro · PostGIS · GeoPandas · OSMNX · NetworkX ·  
HPC / SLURM · Google Earth Engine · Spatial econometrics

## Selected Work
- Large-scale street network analysis and crime modeling
- Longitudinal analysis of urban form (1990–2023)
- Parcel- and subdivision-level spatial clustering
- Geospatial automation pipelines for local governments
